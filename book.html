Look beyond the surface

Why do some people command attention while others struggle to be heard? How does manipulation work? And how can you recognize when someone is influencing your thinking without your awareness?

In Unfolding Rules of Power, Jayash Rajawat explores the psychology of power, human behaviour, influence, and manipulation.

What you will explore

  • Recognize patterns of manipulation and influence.
  • Understand the psychology behind people's actions.
  • Communicate with greater awareness and confidence.
  • Set boundaries and make thoughtful decisions.
  • Understand power dynamics in everyday relationships.

Power is not simply about controlling others. It is also about understanding yourself, thinking independently, and choosing how you respond.

Whether you are curious about psychology, human behaviour, or personal growth, this book offers a thought-provoking journey into the forces that shape the way people think, act, and interact.
