The Unfolding Rules Creator Hub is the creator affiliate program for Unfolding Rules of Power.

How the program works

Creators receive a personal referral link they can share with their audience. The hub tracks referral clicks, records independently verified book sales and calculates creator commissions.

Creators can view clicks, verified sales, earnings and payout status from the creator dashboard. The minimum withdrawal is ₹100, and payout requests become eligible after 2 days.
