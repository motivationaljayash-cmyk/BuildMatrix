Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Unfolding Rules Creator Hub (“the Hub”), operated by Jayash Singh Rajawat, handles information when you browse the website or use the creator affiliate program. It is written in plain language and should be read together with the creator program terms.

1. Information we handle

  • Account information: name, email address, account identifier and account/security events needed to run sign-in. Password authentication is handled by Supabase Auth; the Hub does not need to display your password.
  • Payout information: the UPI ID or bank account holder, account number, IFSC and bank name that a creator chooses to submit to request a payout.
  • Referral analytics: referral or campaign code, click time, campaign/link identifiers, a random visitor identifier stored in your browser’s local storage, a hashed version of that identifier for click deduplication, browser user-agent and referring page/domain. Public information pages may also record the page path, visit time and referring domain.
  • Affiliate and payment records: creator-specific attribution tag/report identifiers, tagged destination URLs, imported Amazon Attribution report metrics, recorded eligible sales, commission amounts, withdrawal requests, payment references and any documented refund-related adjustment.
  • Messages: information you send to our support email when you ask a question or request help with your data or account.

2. Why we use this information

We use this information to operate creator accounts; generate and attribute referral links; count and reduce duplicate clicks; distinguish referral traffic from verified sales; calculate commissions and payable balances; process and document payouts; detect misuse; respond to requests; and maintain business, accounting and security records.

3. Referral clicks are not proof of a sale

The Hub records a referral click separately from an Amazon purchase. Sale attribution is intended to use a unique Amazon Attribution tag/report identifier for a creator and an official Amazon Attribution report imported by the program administrator. At present, report import is manual; the automatic Amazon Ads API sync is not enabled. Until a creator-specific tag and tagged destination URL are configured, the click count alone cannot verify an Amazon purchase.

Amazon Attribution reports generally provide aggregated campaign/ad-group metrics rather than a customer’s identity or a complete order-level refund ledger. Therefore, the Hub cannot always identify which individual creator’s attributed unit was later refunded. A refund-related commission adjustment is made only after independent review and documentation, where there is sufficient evidence to attribute it to the creator. Clicks alone are never treated as sales.

4. Local storage and technical data

The website uses browser local storage for a random visitor identifier and a short page-view cooldown to reduce repeated analytics events. The referral redirect uses that identifier to deduplicate repeat clicks and sends a hash of it to the database. The current browser click handler does not intentionally send a raw IP address to the affiliate database; hosting or infrastructure providers may maintain their own standard network/security logs under their respective policies. You can clear local storage in your browser settings, although this may affect click deduplication.

5. When information is shared

We do not sell creator account or payout information. Information is shared only as needed to operate the Hub, verify referrals, or make payments, including with:

  • Supabase: authentication and database services for creator accounts, analytics and payout records. The project is currently configured in the Australia (Sydney) region, so information may be stored or processed outside India.
  • Render: website hosting and technical delivery.
  • Amazon: when you follow a book or referral link, your visit is handled by Amazon. If attribution tags are configured, Amazon processes the tagged visit under Amazon’s own terms and privacy notice.
  • Payment providers: UPI apps, banks or payment networks used to carry out a payout. They process payment details under their own policies.
  • Website dependencies: the site loads the Supabase JavaScript client from a content delivery provider. That provider may receive technical request data required to deliver the library.

We may disclose records if required by law or needed to protect the Hub, creators or other users from fraud or abuse.

6. Retention and security

We keep information for as long as an account and the program relationship remain active, and for a reasonable period afterward when needed to resolve disputes, maintain payout/accounting records, meet legal obligations or protect the service. We aim to limit access to account and payout information, but no website or storage system can guarantee absolute security. Do not send passwords by email.

7. Your requests and choices

You can contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with your account. You can also ask questions about how information is used or request account closure. We may need to retain limited commission, payout or audit records where legally required or necessary to resolve outstanding obligations. Clearing browser local storage can stop the local visitor identifier from being reused by that browser.

8. Children and creator accounts

The creator program is intended for people who are legally able to participate and receive payouts. People under 18 should not create a creator account or submit payout details. If you believe an account or payout details were submitted by a minor without appropriate parent/guardian involvement, contact us so we can review the matter.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Hub’s systems or practices change. The latest version and effective date will be published on this page. Material changes will be highlighted on the website where reasonably practical.

10. Contact for privacy questions

For privacy requests, data corrections, account closure, or questions about referral tracking, email motivationaljayash@gmail.com. Include enough detail for us to identify the relevant account, but do not send your password or unnecessary bank details.

This is a plain-language policy for the current service. It is not legal advice and should be reviewed against the final operating arrangements and applicable laws before the program is expanded.
